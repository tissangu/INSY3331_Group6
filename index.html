Responsible AI & Legal Compliance Notice
GetShifty optimizes shift allocations using algorithmic constraint-satisfaction heuristics based solely on qualifications, availability, and hour caps. Fairness is treated as an optimization objective, not an absolute guarantee. Managers remain responsible for validating schedules against applicable local labor regulations, mandatory meal and rest periods, and contractual agreements.
