🧠 How TripSplit AI Makes Decisions

1. Lowest-Budget Anchoring

Group activities and shared expenses are calculated against the friend with the lowest stated budget, preventing uncomfortable social friction or exclusion.

2. Democratic Consensus Voting

Every activity is scored based on the percentage of travelers who selected its category. High-consensus activities are scheduled first.

3. Minority Preference Guarantee

To avoid majority tyranny, the engine tracks fulfilled interests per person and reserves slots for minority tags (e.g. vintage shopping or quiet scenic views).

4. 100% Privacy & Local Storage

No financial data, logins, or tracking cookies are sent over the internet. Everything is computed in your browser and saved to your device's localStorage.
